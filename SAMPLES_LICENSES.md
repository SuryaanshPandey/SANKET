# Third-Party Sample Data

The following sample receipt images are derived from the ICDAR 2019 SROIE (Scanned Receipts OCR and Information Extraction) dataset:

- samples/diverse/receipts/01_sroie_grocery_receipt.jpg
- samples/diverse/receipts/02_sroie_retail_pos.jpg
- samples/diverse/receipts/03_sroie_supermarket_tax.jpg

SROIE is an ICDAR 2019 document-analysis dataset. The referenced dataset mirror identifies the dataset as distributed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License (CC BY 4.0).

Source:
https://github.com/jsdnrs/ICDAR2019-SROIE

The remaining SANKET sample documents should be treated as project demonstration assets and are not represented as real law-enforcement records.
